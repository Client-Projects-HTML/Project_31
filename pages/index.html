About Grand Palace

Twelve years of turning ordinary dates into palace evenings.

Grand Palace opened in 2014 with one hall and a promise: every guest should feel like royalty for a night. Today we run six venues across the city, from intimate courtyards to a 1,200-seat grand hall.

Our mission is stress-free celebrations. Our vision is to be the first name Hyderabad thinks of for a wedding, reception or milestone birthday.
