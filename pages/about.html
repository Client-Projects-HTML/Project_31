About Grand Palace

Welcome to Grand Palace, where tradition meets modern luxury. Discover the story of our heritage and the passionate team dedicated to making your celebrations unforgettable.

Our Heritage

A Legacy of Grandeur

Founded in 2014, Grand Palace was born from a singular vision: to bring the architectural majesty of Jaipur's royal courts to the heart of Hyderabad. We believed that a celebration is not just an event; it is a milestone in your family's history, and it deserves a setting that reflects its importance.

What started as a single 300-guest hall has blossomed into a magnificent six-venue estate. From the intricate stone carvings of the Rajwada Courtyard to the sweeping, pillarless expanse of the Maharani Grand Hall, every inch of our property has been designed to evoke a sense of awe and timeless elegance.
