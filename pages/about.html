Our Story

Twelve years of building rooms worth celebrating in.

Company Story

Started with one hall and a stubborn idea

In 2014, three friends converted a single wedding lawn into Grand Palace's first hall. The idea was simple and, at the time, unusual: treat every booking — birthday or wedding — with the same craft a five-star hotel gives a state banquet.

That idea grew into six venues, an in-house decoration studio, and a catering bench trusted by the city's best chefs.

Vision

To be the first name Hyderabad thinks of for a celebration.

Not the biggest hall in the city — the one hosts recommend without being asked.

Mission

Stress-free celebrations, from enquiry to the last dance.

One point of contact, transparent pricing, and a team that treats your date like it's the only one on the calendar.
