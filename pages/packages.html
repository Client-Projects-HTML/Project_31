Package Details FAQ

Can we customize a package?

Absolutely. While our packages offer bundled value, our concierge can add or remove elements to perfectly suit your vision and budget.

Do prices include taxes?

Prices listed are base prices. An 18% GST is applicable on all venue and service bookings as per government regulations.

What is the payment schedule?

We require a 30% advance to secure your date, 50% one month prior to the event, and the remaining 20% one week before the event date.
